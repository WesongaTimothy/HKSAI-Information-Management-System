Welcome to HKSAI

HKSAI empowers adolescents living in Kenya’s informal settlements through education, health programs, skills training, and community advocacy. Join us in creating a brighter future for young people.

Vision

A society where inspired young people lead independent, dignified, and healthy lives.

Mission

To advance education, health, and socioeconomic opportunities that empower adolescents and youths in Kenya’s informal settlements.

Our Values

Humanity

We act with compassion to improve lives.

Excellence

We deliver high-quality programs and services.

Integrity

We uphold transparency, honesty, and accountability.

Respect

We value every individual and community we serve.

Professionalism

We maintain high standards in all our operations.

Fairness

We treat everyone equitably and justly.

Our Programs

Education Support

Providing mentorship, tuition support, and learning resources to adolescents.

Health Programs

Promoting adolescent health, menstrual hygiene, and awareness initiatives.

Skills & Empowerment

Vocational training, digital literacy, and entrepreneurship support.

Advocacy & Community

Strengthening community structures and influencing policy for adolescent rights.
