About HKSAI

Empowering youth and transforming lives across Kenya's informal settlements.

Who We Are

Hope for Kenya Slum Adolescents Initiative (HKSAI) is a grassroots, youth-focused non-profit organization dedicated to enhancing education, health outcomes, and socio-economic opportunities for vulnerable adolescents living in informal settlement communities across Kenya.

Our Enduring Mission

To advance education and health access for adolescents and youth living in Kenya's informal settings through community-led capacity building, advocacy, and strategic partnerships that foster lifelong learning, health equity, and sustainable empowerment.

Our 2030 Vision

By 2030, HKSAI will strengthen its internal capacity, governance, and financial stability to advance inclusive education and health access for youth in Kenya's informal settlements—building a world of resilient, empowered youth leading independent, dignified, and healthy lives.

We center compassion and empathy, recognizing the inherent dignity, worth, and potential of every individual regardless of their background or circumstance.

02. Innovation

03. Inclusiveness

We are committed to equity, representation, and belonging—ensuring that every voice, especially marginalized youth and adolescent girls, is heard and valued.

05. Partnership

Strategic Roadmap (2026–2030)

Our 5-year framework structured around four key operational pillars:

1. Organizational Capacity

Strengthening governance, internal systems, workflows, data management, and operational policies to support scalable growth.

2. Programs & Impact

Sharpening and expanding youth-centered education, mental wellness, health advocacy, and livelihood programs.

3. Strategic Partnerships

Cultivating multisectoral collaborations with government entities, peer NGOs, and networks to amplify reach and influence policy.

4. Financial Sustainability

Diversifying funding streams, establishing earned-income models, building operational reserves, and ensuring resilience.

2030 Horizon Targets & Milestones

  • Education Expansion: Scale educational beneficiary support from 157 to 550 scholars with a target 100% post-secondary transition rate.
  • Post-Secondary Employment: Ensure 80% of post-secondary graduates secure employment or economic opportunities within 6 months.
  • Health Outreach: Five-fold expansion of health programs reaching 40,000 beneficiaries with 90% positive health behavior outcomes.
  • Financial Diversification: Shift non-grant income to 60% of total operational budget while building structural financial reserves.
