Melvine Praxides Ouyo

Founder & Executive Director

Melvine Ouyo is the Founder and Executive Director of Hope for Kenya Slum Adolescents Initiative (HKSAI), an organization established to reverse structural inequalities and empower adolescent girls living in urban informal settlements through education, health, vocational training, and economic youth empowerment.

She is a seasoned Public Health and Policy expert with over two decades of experience advancing health equity, gender justice, and community development in Kenya. Growing up, Melvine witnessed firsthand the structural biases that exposed women and girls in her community to gender-based violence, forced marriages, teenage pregnancies, early childbirth, and limited access to education. These experiences shaped her lifelong commitment to protecting the rights of women and girls and ensuring that the most marginalized communities are not left behind.

Melvine brings extensive experience as a reproductive healthcare provider and reproductive health and rights advocate, having worked closely with vulnerable, marginalized, and hard-to-reach populations across Kenya. Her work bridges community-level realities with policy-level solutions, focusing on global health, health policy reform, women and girls’ empowerment, and sustainable community development.

She is a Mid-Career Master’s graduate of the Harvard Kennedy School, where she strengthened her expertise in public policy, leadership, and evidence-based solutions for complex social challenges. Through HKSAI, Melvine continues to champion inclusive policies and grassroots interventions that create lasting change for adolescent girls and their communities.
