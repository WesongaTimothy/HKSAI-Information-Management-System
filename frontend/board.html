Melvine Praxides Ouyo

Founder & Executive Director

Melvine Praxides Ouyo is a seasoned Public Health and Policy expert with two decades of experience. A Mid-Career Master’s graduate from the Harvard Kennedy School, she founded HKSAI to reverse the structural biases that expose women and girls to gender-based violence, forced marriage, and educational inequity.

Drawing from her background as a reproductive health care provider and advocate, Melvine focuses on global health policy reforms and community development. Her mission is to empower Kenya’s most vulnerable communities by building sustainable pathways through education, health, and vocational empowerment.
