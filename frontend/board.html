About the Founder

Melvine Praxides Ouyo

Melvine Ouyo founded HKSAI to reverse structural biases affecting women and girls in Kenya. Drawing from her experience as a reproductive health provider and rights advocate, she focuses on health policy reforms and community development to empower the most vulnerable communities.
