Our Reaffirmed Core Values

01 Humanity

We believe in the inherent dignity, worth, and potential of every individual. We center compassion and empathy, treating every adolescent with care and respect regardless of background.

02 Innovation

We embrace bold ideas and creative solutions. We seek smarter, adaptive approaches to meet evolving needs, challenging norms and pioneering new pathways for impact.

03 Inclusiveness

Committed to equity, representation, and belonging. We ensure every voice, especially marginalized girls and youth, is heard, valued, and reflected in our programs.

04 Integrity

We uphold honesty, transparency, and accountability. We act with courage and consistency, building trust with donors and communities through ethical practices.

05 Partnership

We thrive through collective action. We believe lasting impact is achieved by working hand-in-hand with community leaders, public institutions, and changemakers.

06 Collaboration

Harnessing our collective strengths for the greater good. We foster a culture of shared purpose to advance inclusive education and health access.

Our Enduring Mission

HKSAI exists to advance equitable access to education and healthcare for adolescents and youths living in Kenya’s informal settlements through community-led capacity building and strategic partnerships.
