Our Mission

HKSAI exists to advance equitable access to education and healthcare for adolescents and youths living in Kenya’s informal settlements. We achieve this through community-led capacity building, advocacy, and strategic partnerships that foster lifelong learning, health equity, and sustainable empowerment.

Our Core Values

Humanity

Centering compassion and empathy, treating every adolescent with dignity, regardless of background.

Innovation

Embracing bold ideas and creative solutions to meet the evolving needs of underserved communities.

Inclusiveness

Commitment to equity, ensuring the voices of marginalized girls and youth are heard and valued.

Integrity

Upholding honesty and transparency, building trust through ethical practices and principled leadership.

Collaboration

Achieving lasting impact by working hand-in-hand with community leaders and partners.

Excellence

Maintaining high standards of professionalism and fairness in all programmatic outcomes.

HKSAI 2030

Leading the way in adolescent empowerment across Kenya's informal settlements. Our 2030 strategy focuses on scaling impact for 40,000 health beneficiaries and 400 scholars.
