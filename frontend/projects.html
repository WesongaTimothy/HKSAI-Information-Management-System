Our Core Initiatives

HKSAI implements sustainable programs designed to address the unique challenges faced by adolescents in urban settlements through a holistic 360° approach.

Academic Scholarship Program

Using a 360° approach, we provide tuition, books, uniforms, and shoes to eliminate financial barriers for bright and needy students.

197 Girls Enrolled as of 2025

HKSAI Training Centre (TVET)

Located in Kibera, Laini Saba, offering Beauty, Hairdressing, and Computer Operations to provide job-ready skills.

Goal: 300 Graduates by 2030

Menstrual Health & Hygiene

Breaking period stigma and promoting dignity through professional health talks and distribution of hygiene kits.

20,000 Pads Distributed Yearly

Economic Empowerment (WEDU)

Providing social entrepreneurial support, vocational skills, and resources for youth to launch small businesses.

WEDU & SCRA Projects Active

SRHR & Advocacy

Promoting sexual rights and advocating against gender-based violence, early marriages, and harmful cultural practices.

10,000 Girls Trained Annually

Mental Health & Counseling

Safe spaces for youth to discuss trauma, mental health stigma, and coping strategies for school and relationships.
